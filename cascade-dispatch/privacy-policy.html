Privacy Policy

Cascade Dispatch: Signal Crisis · Publisher: GoodUse Studios · Effective: August 18, 2026

This policy explains how Cascade Dispatch: Signal Crisis (“Cascade Dispatch,” “the game,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the Windows version distributed through Microsoft Store.

Short version: the game does not require a Cascade Dispatch account. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Microsoft Store handles the lifetime purchase. The free version may use contextual, non-personalized advertising.

1. Information stored by the game

Cascade Dispatch stores game data locally on your Windows device so the game can function and remember your progress. This may include:

  • campaign progress, clears, streaks, records and difficulty/progression state;
  • audio, language and other game settings;
  • attempt-recharge timestamps and related local timing state; and
  • a local counter used to control how often advertisements may be requested.

This game data is not uploaded to a GoodUse Studios gameplay server, and the game does not require you to create a Cascade Dispatch account.

2. Microsoft Store purchases

The optional “Unlimited Play” lifetime unlock is purchased and licensed through Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes the transaction and maintains the Microsoft-account purchase and license records. Cascade Dispatch does not collect or store your payment-card number, bank details, or Microsoft account password.

The Windows app checks Microsoft Store licensing information to determine whether the lifetime unlock is owned. For offline continuity, the app may keep a device-local, Windows-protected record of the most recent Store entitlement result. Microsoft Store remains authoritative when a live license check is available.

3. Advertising in free mode

The free version may display clearly identified advertisements outside the active 30-second puzzle decision period. Cascade Dispatch requests contextual, non-personalized advertising only and does not request the Windows advertising ID.

To deliver, secure, limit frequency, troubleshoot and measure advertisements, the third-party advertising service used in the shipping build may automatically process information such as an IP address, app version, device or operating-system information, approximate region derived from network information, ad-delivery events, error/diagnostic information and interactions with an advertisement. The exact data processed is controlled by the selected advertising service and its own privacy terms.

Cascade Dispatch does not intentionally request interest-based advertising. You can also control the Windows advertising ID through Windows privacy settings. If the advertising service provides additional privacy or opt-out controls, those controls also apply.

4. How information is used

Information handled through the game is used only to operate the game, preserve local progress and settings, enforce the free-mode attempt timer, verify Microsoft Store ownership, display and measure contextual advertising, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and maintain security.

5. Sharing and sale of information

GoodUse Studios does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Microsoft in connection with Microsoft Store licensing and purchases, and by the advertising service used to deliver free-mode ads. Those services act under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the security of the product, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

6. Retention and deletion

Device-local game data remains on the device until it is removed by the game, reset by you where a reset function is provided, cleared through Windows/app-data controls, or removed when the application and its local data are deleted. Microsoft Store transaction and license records are retained by Microsoft under Microsoft’s policies. Advertising-service retention is governed by the selected advertising provider.

7. Security

The app is designed to minimize data collection. Paid entitlement state used for offline continuity is stored using Windows user-scoped data protection in the packaged Windows host. Network traffic used by Microsoft Store and the selected advertising service is handled by those services and their SDKs.

8. Children

Cascade Dispatch is not specifically directed to children under 13. The game’s final age ratings are determined through the IARC questionnaire in Microsoft Partner Center. If a release is designated as directed to children in a jurisdiction, advertising and data practices must be configured to meet the applicable child-privacy requirements before that release is made available there.

9. International processing

Microsoft Store and the selected advertising provider may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable safeguards and privacy terms.

10. Your choices

  • You may use the paid lifetime unlock to remove advertising and the free-mode attempt limit.
  • You may control Windows advertising-ID settings through Windows privacy settings.
  • You may remove device-local game data using Windows/app-data controls or by uninstalling the game and removing its local data.
  • You may contact the publisher using the support/contact channel shown on the Cascade Dispatch: Signal Crisis Microsoft Store listing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the product, advertising provider, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

12. Contact

Publisher: GoodUse Studios. For privacy questions or requests, use the publisher support/contact channel displayed on the Microsoft Store product listing for Cascade Dispatch: Signal Crisis.
