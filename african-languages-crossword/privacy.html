African Languages Crossword

Privacy Policy
Effective October 31, 2026

African Languages Crossword is a crossword and word-game app featuring African languages and varieties including Pidgin, Swahili, isiZulu, Krio, Kriolu, Kamtok, and related packs.

Information handled by the app

WorksBien Studios does not require an account and does not ask players to submit a name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

The app stores gameplay information on the device, such as the selected game language, crossword progress, completed puzzles, hints, recent words, mastery progress, and related preferences. This local information is used to operate the game and can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and mediation

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob mediation may allow Google and third-party ad sources configured by WorksBien Studios in AdMob to compete to serve an advertisement.

Depending on the device, region, consent choices, and ad source, Google and participating advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • advertising or device identifiers;
  • device, operating-system, app, and language information;
  • ad impressions, clicks, views, rewards, and other interactions;
  • crash, diagnostic, fraud-prevention, and performance information; and
  • consent and privacy signals.

WorksBien Studios does not receive the player's name or contact list from these advertising SDKs. Advertising providers process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations. Available mediation partners can change when ad-source configuration changes.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before requesting advertisements. The privacy shield in the app provides access to this policy and, when required by Google, to privacy choices that allow the player to review or change consent selections.

Google Play services

The app may use Google Play's in-app review service. Google may process information associated with the Play Store and device under the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and sale

WorksBien Studios does not independently sell information that players submit to the app, and the app does not provide WorksBien Studios with an account profile or contact details. Information may be processed by Google and enabled mediation partners to deliver, measure, secure, and personalize or limit advertising as permitted by the player's consent and applicable law.

Children

The app filters inappropriate dictionary content and is designed for a general audience, but it is not directed specifically to children under 13. WorksBien Studios does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account or form. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us.

Retention and deletion

On-device gameplay information remains until the player clears app storage or uninstalls the app. Advertising providers retain information according to their own policies, consent choices, and legal requirements.

Security and international processing

Reasonable measures are used to protect the app and its local data. Advertising providers may process information in countries other than the player's country, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, advertising partners, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
