# Cascade Dispatch Privacy Policy — source copy

Canonical public URL: https://lrodeveloperr.github.io/privacy-policy/cascade-dispatch/privacy/

Cascade Dispatch stores campaign progress and settings locally on the device and does not require a GoodUse Studios gameplay account. Free mode may use Google AdMob after applicable consent/privacy controls; the app requests non-personalized advertising and does not use gameplay choices to build an advertising profile. Apple or Google processes the lifetime in-app purchase and supplies entitlement information required to verify access. The public policy controls if this source copy differs from the published page.
