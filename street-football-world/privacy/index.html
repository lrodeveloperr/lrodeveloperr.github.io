Street Football World: Soccer – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Street Football World: Soccer (package com.worksbienstudios.monkeypost), published by WorksBien Studios Inc. ("we"), handles and why. The game has no accounts and no chat. It does not ask for your name, email address or contacts.

Information kept on your device

Your game progress, settings, coins and the age band you choose on the first screen are stored only on your device. Gameplay statistics are written to a local file on your device and are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos, short ads between matches and a banner on the menu screens. There are no ads during a match. Ads only start after the age screen, and never for a player who says they are under 13.

When ads are shown, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, information about how you interact with ads, diagnostic information and an approximate location inferred from your IP address, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We ask Google for non-personalised ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (remove ads, a starter pack and coin bundles) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The game keeps a purchase record on your device so it can grant and restore what you bought.

Playing with a nearby friend

Nearby and Wi-Fi hotspot matches connect two phones directly. Only game moves are exchanged between the two phones; nothing is sent to us.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. A player who chooses an under-13 age band sees no ads, and the ad SDK is not started for them.

Your choices and requests

You can change your ad consent under Settings → Privacy choices (where it applies), turn off personalised advertising in your Android settings, or delete all game data by uninstalling the game. For any question or request about your information, contact us at info@worksbienstudios.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
