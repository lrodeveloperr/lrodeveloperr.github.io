2. Data used by the beta

Data kept on your device

  • level number, recent first-clear records, tutorial and progress state;
  • free and earned attempt balances, reset anchor, local clock-integrity markers, and active-attempt recovery state;
  • language, appearance, accessibility, audio, haptic, and confirmation settings;
  • a coarse local experience route (adult or protected). The app does not request a birth date, identity document, selfie, or legal name.

This data is used only to operate the game, preserve deterministic progress, detect a material device-clock jump before applying a free-attempt reset, apply fair attempt rules, and keep store content away from the protected route. It remains in the app sandbox until you use Reset local progress, clear app data, or uninstall. The Android beta explicitly excludes this state from cloud backup and device transfer. On iOS, an operating-system backup may include app-scoped state according to your device and iCloud backup settings; deleting the app’s local state does not itself delete an older device backup managed by Apple.

Data we receive automatically

None from core beta gameplay. The beta does not initialize a purchase SDK or commerce service and contains no analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, remote-configuration, authentication, or social SDK.
