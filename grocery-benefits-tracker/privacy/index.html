Privacy Policy

Effective date and version: August 13, 2026

Grocery Benefits Tracker is operated by Lateef Razaq-Oyetola, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. On Google Play, the app may be published under the developer name GoodUse Studios, the Android-facing developer name used by the operator.

Privacy summaryIndependent local-first tracker. No account, profile, or publisher-operated analytics or telemetry. Core tracker data is stored in the app on this device and is not uploaded to an operator-controlled server; exports and device backups are explained below. On iOS, without a verified one-time Remove Ads entitlement, the app may request one fixed non-personalized banner when Google permits an ad request. On Android, without a verified one-time Remove Ads entitlement, the app may request one fixed non-personalized banner on eligible screens after the legal gate and Google's required privacy process when Google permits an ad request. Google may process device and advertising data as explained below. Privacy Choices manages Google's applicable advertising-data choices; it is not an ad-visibility or purchase control. The app never asks for an EBT/WIC PIN or connects to a government benefit account.

1. Information stored in the tracker

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter SNAP/PAN balances; WIC benefits, quantities, categories, brands, start and expiration dates; grocery items, quantities and prices; store names; payment or benefit allocations; shopping-budget amounts and periods; transaction history; saved baskets; reminder settings; and app preferences.

These tracker records are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to an operator-controlled account, analytics service, profile database, or cloud database. The app does not send your locally entered balances, benefits, grocery items, budgets, or History as advertising parameters.

2. Identity and credentials the tracker does not request

The tracker does not create or maintain a personal profile. It has no dedicated fields for your name, case number, agency, caseworker or clinic name, street or mailing address, Social Security number, email address, phone number, date of birth, full EBT/WIC card number, EBT PIN, WIC PIN, or other case-routing identity information.

The app does not require a SNAP, PAN, EBT, or WIC account login and does not connect to government benefit accounts. Do not enter PINs, passwords, Social Security numbers, full benefit-card numbers, or full payment-card numbers into free-text labels.

3. Reports, exports, backups, and imports

Reports are created locally and are designed without built-in participant identity, personal-profile, or case-routing fields. They can still contain sensitive dates, stores, items, quantities, prices, benefit allocations, split payments, corrections, refunds, imported-record provenance, reconciliation information, warnings, and app identification. Review and protect a report before sharing it. App-generated reports are supporting records, not official agency forms or government records.

Export and import occur only when you initiate them. The app reads compatible imports locally and does not upload History to an operator-controlled server. Imported records retain their source provenance and do not automatically modify the receiving device's current SNAP, WIC, or shopping-budget balances.

The iOS and Android apps may temporarily write an export to local app-cache storage so the system share interface can present it, and they attempt to delete that temporary copy shortly afterward. Clear All Data removes a surviving app-cache copy, but it does not delete copies you saved or shared to separate locations. The destination you select—such as Files, email, messaging, cloud storage, an agency, or another app—is a separate third party whose terms and privacy practices apply. The operator cannot retrieve a forgotten encrypted-backup passphrase. Anyone with access to an unencrypted export may be able to read it.

4. Advertising and store privacy disclosures

The app's core tracker features are free. The app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK under platform-specific advertising rules. On iOS, an ad request is made only when the app does not have a verified Remove Ads entitlement and Google reports that an ad request is permitted. On Android, after the versioned legal gate, an ad request is made only when there is no verified Remove Ads entitlement and Google's UMP privacy process reports that an ad request is permitted. Google may process information needed to request, deliver, secure, frequency-cap, measure, and improve ads. Depending on the device, consent choice, jurisdiction, SDK configuration, and ad response, this may include IP address and coarse location inferred from it; device or app identifiers; app launches and advertising-related product interactions; advertising data; crash data; performance data; and other diagnostic data.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect Device ID for third-party advertising and analytics. Consistent with the SDK’s privacy manifest, Device ID is disclosed in Apple’s App Privacy information as data used for tracking. The app requests non-personalized ads; its own code does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization or directly access IDFA.

Because an ad request originates from this app, Google can know that the request or interaction came from a SNAP/PAN and WIC tracker, which may permit an inference about the device user's interests. The app does not send your locally entered balances, benefits, grocery items, budgets, or History as ad parameters.

The app does not include a separate publisher-operated analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDK. Store privacy disclosures for a release must accurately assess at least Coarse Location, Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, Crash Data, Performance Data, and Other Diagnostic Data against the packaged SDK and current platform definitions. Apple may classify some categories as linked to a user through a device identifier even though the app has no named account and the operator does not combine advertising data with local benefit records. For Android, Google states that the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance, using TLS in transit. See the Google Mobile Ads Android data disclosure, Google Mobile Ads iOS data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and Apple App Privacy Details.

5. Advertising configuration and choices

The app is configured to request non-personalized ads. The iOS version does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally access IDFA for personalized advertising. On iOS, publisher first-party ID is disabled. The current Android build does not separately disable Google Mobile Ads publisher first-party ID, which Google enables by default; each Android banner request is nevertheless configured as non-personalized. The iOS package may include SKAdNetwork identifiers for Apple's privacy-preserving attribution framework. Non-personalized advertising does not mean no technical advertising data is processed.

iOS. The app updates Google's UMP consent information before requesting an ad and presents any required privacy message. Where available, Privacy Choices reopens Google's separate controls and changes the advertising-data choices available on this device. It is not an ad on/off switch and does not purchase or restore Remove Ads. Without a verified Remove Ads entitlement, the app may request one fixed non-personalized banner only when Google reports that an ad request is permitted.

Android. After you accept the versioned Terms and acknowledge this Privacy Policy, the app refreshes Google's UMP consent information and presents any privacy message Google requires. Without a verified one-time Remove Ads entitlement, it may request one fixed non-personalized banner on eligible screens only when Google reports that ads may be requested. Privacy Choices reopens Google's required advertising-data controls when available; it is not an ad on/off switch and does not buy, restore, or simulate Remove Ads. An ad or privacy-service failure does not block the core tracker.

You may also use operating-system privacy controls, clear tracker data, uninstall the app, or email the operator about support information under the operator's control. We use integrated third-party services only subject to terms requiring them to protect user data consistently with applicable law and the protections required by applicable store rules.

6. Purchases

iOS. Remove Ads Forever is a one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase. The U.S. list price is US$9.99; before you confirm, the App Store shows the price and currency that apply to your storefront. It is not a subscription and does not unlock or restrict the tracker's core features. Purchase it from Remove Ads in the sidebar. To recover a previous purchase made with the same Apple Account, use Settings → Restore Purchase; restoring does not charge you again. Banner ads remain hidden only while Apple reports a valid purchase entitlement. A refund, revocation, or other loss of entitlement may cause ads to return.

Apple processes the account, billing, and payment information for the iOS purchase under Apple's policies. StoreKit provides the app with product information and verified transaction or entitlement status needed to offer, validate, complete, and restore the purchase. Android. Remove Ads Forever is a one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase with product ID remove_ads_lifetime. It is not a subscription and does not unlock or restrict core tracker features. The app displays Google Play's localized price, lets you purchase from Remove Ads, and provides Settings → Restore Purchase for the same Google Play account. Ads remain hidden only while Google Play reports a valid purchased entitlement; a pending purchase does not remove ads, and a refund, revocation, suspension, or other loss of entitlement may cause ads to return. Google Play processes the account, billing, payment, tax, refund, and purchase-history information and provides the app with product, purchase, entitlement, and acknowledgement status. The app processes that status locally and does not send a Play purchase token or purchase status to an operator-controlled backend. The app does not receive full payment-card details.

7. Local notifications

If you enable a reminder, the app asks for notification permission when the platform requires it and schedules the reminder locally on this device. On Android 13 or later, this includes the Android notification permission. The app does not use an operator-controlled push-notification server and does not send a push token to one. Notification titles and messages may be visible on the lock screen according to your device settings and may reveal that you use SNAP/PAN or WIC. You can disable reminders in the app or notifications in system settings. Clearing all app data cancels this app's scheduled local reminders.

A support link may open your email app. If you voluntarily send an email, your provider sends the address, technical details, attachments, and content you choose to provide to the operator outside the app. Do not include benefit credentials, PINs, Social Security numbers, full card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.

Support correspondence is hosted through Gmail/Google and may be processed in countries where Google operates. It is used to answer the request, investigate defects or abuse, keep necessary business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. 9. Official sources and external pages

The app does not include a Benefits & Resources directory. It may open Official Government Sources and legal, support, Apple, or Google pages. When you open an external page, its operator may process information under its own policy. We do not control those external practices.

10. Retention and deletion

Core tracker records remain locally stored until you change or delete them. Settings → Clear All Data permanently removes this app's locally stored tracker data from this device, removes current and recognized legacy/recovery tracker state and surviving temporary export-cache copies, cancels this app's scheduled local reminders, and returns you to initial setup. Files you previously saved or shared to separate locations are not deleted.

Clear All Data does not cancel, refund, delete, or simulate an App Store or Google Play Remove Ads purchase or entitlement; the applicable store manages it separately. Clearing tracker data does not revoke system notification permission or necessarily erase Google's separately stored advertising-consent record. Revoke notification permission in system settings and use Privacy Choices where available. Because there is no app account, there is no operator-hosted tracker account to delete. Delete exported copies from their destination yourself.

11. Privacy Mode, device storage, and backups

Privacy Mode only masks selected values on the screen. It does not add separate encryption beyond the protections provided by the operating system to local storage, exported files, device backups, or notification content.

The Android release disables standard Android app-data backup. Manufacturer device-transfer behavior, iOS or device migration, and separately saved exports may still vary or remain outside the operator's control. The operator cannot access or delete separately retained copies; use your platform's backup, device-storage, and deletion controls to manage them.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Information processed by Apple, Google, email providers, share destinations, or external sites is also subject to their safeguards.

Security incidents. We maintain procedures to assess and respond to a breach of safeguards involving personal information under the operator's control. We keep the records required by law for at least 24 months and, where the breach creates a real risk of significant harm, report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and notify affected people as soon as feasible, as required by law.
