Privacy

Delete your data

Updated August 13, 2026

No app accountGrocery Benefits Tracker has no app account, login, or operator-hosted tracker profile. Core tracker records are stored locally on your device. The operator cannot see or remotely delete those local records.

Delete local tracker data in the app

  1. Open the app menu.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Clear All Data.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm Clear All Data.

This permanently removes this app's locally stored tracker state from that device, including setup and preferences; locally entered SNAP/PAN and WIC records and balances; grocery items, baskets, budgets, transactions, History, corrections, and report settings; recognized legacy or recovery tracker state; and surviving temporary export-cache copies. It cancels this app's scheduled local reminders and returns the app to initial setup.

You can also remove local app data through Android system storage controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

What Clear All Data does not delete

  • Files you previously exported, saved, emailed, uploaded, or shared to another app, device, cloud provider, or destination. Delete those copies at their destination.
  • A Google Play or App Store Remove Ads purchase, entitlement, purchase history, refund record, or store-account record. The applicable store controls those records.
  • System notification permission. Revoke it in your device settings.
  • Google's separately stored advertising-consent choices or advertising data. Use Privacy Choices in the app when available and Google's privacy controls.
  • Support email or other information you voluntarily sent to the operator.

Request deletion of operator-controlled information

The operator does not receive your local tracker records. If you previously contacted support, you may request deletion of applicable support correspondence or other personal information controlled by the operator by emailing lrodeveloperr@gmail.com with the subject Privacy Request. We may request limited information to verify and locate the request.

Support correspondence is normally deleted or de-identified within 24 months after the last substantive contact unless a longer period is reasonably required for an active dispute, security incident, fraud prevention, or legal obligation. We respond to a privacy request no later than 30 days unless a lawful extension or exception applies. If time is extended, we will notify you and explain your complaint rights.

Third-party records

Google Play, AdMob, Apple, GitHub, email providers, export destinations, and other third parties control their own records. The operator cannot delete data that it does not possess or control. Use the relevant provider's privacy, account, purchase, and deletion controls. See the Privacy Policy for details.
